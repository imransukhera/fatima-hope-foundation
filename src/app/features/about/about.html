About Us

Empowering Underprivileged Communities in Pakistan

Fatima Hope Foundation is a non-profit welfare organization dedicated to restoring dignity, self-reliance, and hope across Bahawalnagar, Fort Abbas, Bahawalpur, and Multan through sustainable food security, emergency healthcare, and child education programs.

Our Vision

A Pakistan where no vulnerable family suffers from chronic hunger, no underprivileged child is denied quality schooling, and every community in Southern Punjab receives immediate support in times of crisis.

Our Mission

To uplift low-income households by providing monthly ration bags, critical medical aid, child tuition sponsorships, orphan welfare, and rapid emergency relief across rural and remote communities.

Our Story

Established in 2015 with a single food distribution drive, Fatima Hope Foundation has grown into a trusted humanitarian network serving thousands of deserving families across Pakistan with complete transparency.
